What is AutoClips?

AutoClips is a free Windows desktop app that automatically captures your best gaming moments. It keeps a rolling recording buffer while you play, watches your screen for big moments, and saves a clip the instant they happen — so you never have to remember to hit record.

Features

  • Automatic kill detection — detects kills, knockdowns, and eliminations on screen in Call of Duty: Warzone, Apex Legends, and Fortnite, and saves a clip automatically.
  • Voice-command backup — just say "AutoClip this" and the last moments are saved, even if the game detection missed it.
  • Discord uploads — optionally send clips straight to your Discord channel via a webhook you provide.
  • YouTube uploads — optionally upload clips to your own YouTube channel by signing in with your Google account.
  • Local-first — clips are recorded and stored on your own PC. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on Discord or YouTube uploads.

How it works

Install AutoClips, pick your audio devices in Settings, and press Start. The app records in the background while you play. When it detects a kill or hears your voice command, it saves the last 30 seconds (configurable) as an MP4 on your computer. If you've enabled uploads, the clip is also sent to your Discord channel or your YouTube channel.
